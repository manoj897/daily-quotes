Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Daily Quote Reflections.

Overview

Daily Quote Reflections is a personal reflection app for reading daily quotes, saving reflections, organizing reflections with tags, setting optional reminders, and sharing quotes or reflections through the Android share sheet.

Daily Quote Reflections does not require an account and does not sell user data.

Information Stored On Your Device

Daily Quote Reflections stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Reflections you write
  • Tags you create or attach to reflections
  • Notification reminder settings
  • Cached quote content used to show the daily quote

This local reflection data is not sent to the developer's server. You can remove this local data by deleting reflections in the app, clearing app data in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Information Sent To Third-Party Services

Daily Quote Reflections uses the following third-party services:

  • ZenQuotes, to fetch daily quote content over HTTPS.
  • Firebase Crashlytics, to collect crash reports and diagnostic information that helps improve app reliability.

Crash reports may include technical information such as app version, device model, operating system version, crash stack traces, and diagnostic identifiers. Crash reports are used only to understand and fix app issues.

When the app fetches a quote from ZenQuotes, ZenQuotes may receive standard network request information such as IP address and request metadata as part of operating its service.

Sharing

If you choose to use the share feature, Android opens a share sheet so you can choose another app. The text you choose to share is sent only to the app you select. Daily Quote Reflections does not control how the selected third-party app handles shared content.

Permissions

Daily Quote Reflections may request these Android permissions:

  • Internet access, to fetch daily quote content and send crash diagnostics.
  • Notification permission, to show optional reminder notifications.
  • Alarm and boot-related permissions, to schedule and restore optional reminders after device restart.

Data Retention And Deletion

Reflections, tags, reminder settings, and quote cache data remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

Crash report retention is managed through Firebase Crashlytics settings and Google Firebase services.

Security

Daily Quote Reflections uses HTTPS for network requests where supported by the service provider. Local app data is stored in Android app-specific storage.

Children

Daily Quote Reflections is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the developer contact email listed on the Daily Quote Reflections Google Play Store listing.
