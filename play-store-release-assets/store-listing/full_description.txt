Daily Quotes helps you turn a daily quote into something personal: your own take, your own tags, and a reflection you can return to.

Use it to:
- Read a fresh daily quote
- Write your take privately on your device
- Tag reflections by themes, routines, moods, or parts of life
- Revisit past reflections in a focused detail view
- Set optional daily reminders
- Share quotes with or without your own take

Quotes are prompts. Your reflection is the point. Capture what a quote means to you, tag it to your world, and build a small library of thoughts that can inspire you later.

No account is required.
